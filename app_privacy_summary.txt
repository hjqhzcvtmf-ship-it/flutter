# App Privacy and Data Collection Summary for ChatGPT

## App Overview
- Flutter app for iOS
- Uses Firebase (authentication, analytics, Firestore)
- Uses Stripe for payments
- Has user sign-in
- Collects user email and possibly name

## Data Collected
- **Contact Info:**
  - Email Address (for authentication and communication)
  - Name (if collected during sign-up or profile)
- **Analytics:**
  - Device and usage data (via Firebase Analytics)
- **Payments:**
  - Payment Info (processed by Stripe; app does not store payment details)

## Third-Party Services
- **Firebase:** Authentication, Analytics, Firestore (data storage)
- **Stripe:** Payment processing (PCI-compliant, no sensitive info stored in app)

## Data Usage
- User authentication and account management
- Analytics for app improvement
- Payment processing for in-app purchases or services

## Data Sharing
- Data is shared with Firebase and Stripe for authentication, analytics, and payment processing.
- No data is sold to third parties.
